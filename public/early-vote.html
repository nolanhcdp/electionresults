Howard County Early Vote

2026 general election · early voting Oct 6 – Nov 2 · DNC Dem Party Support, from the daily VAN report · updated Oct 31, 1:15 PM

How to read this. "Dem," "Persuadable" and "Rep" are not how anyone voted. Ballots are secret, so we only know that someone voted, not who they voted for. Each voter is grouped by their DNC support score: a 0 to 100 estimate of how likely they are to support Democrats, built from things like party primary history, demographics and survey data. Dem means a score of 70 or higher, Persuadable 30 to 69.99, Rep under 30. "Dem-leaning" in the comparison charts means 50 or higher. The turnout score works the same way: a 0 to 100 estimate of how likely someone is to vote.

* State Senate and State House districts with an asterisk also cover parts of other counties. Everything on this page counts Howard County voters only, not the full district.

Curve table and method

Indiana's 2024 statewide early-vote curve (share of the final early vote by day of the 28-day window), with Howard County's 2026 count plotted against a reference final. Both pace lines use the timing of Indiana's 2024 statewide early vote (the only year with day-by-day milestones), scaled to Howard County's final early-vote totals from the Secretary of State's turnout reports: 10,104 absentee of 25,096 ballots in 2022 (midterm), and 25,375 of 39,241 in 2024. 2022's own timing may have differed, and the 2024 curve is reconstructed from milestone counts, so treat the first week as rough.
