Pinned races

Pinned races stay on the TV all night and lead everyone's phone. Up to three fit the narrow column; a fourth (or a township board, which takes two slots) switches the TV to a wider pinned block.

Add a race

Pull in any race from around Indiana that has a Democrat and a Republican on the November ballot, or type one in by hand. Races from the state show whole-district results on election night.
